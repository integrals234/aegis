## 1. What M1 was for

M1 builds the deterministic, single-instrument limit-order-book and matching
core every later milestone replays against: a sequencer independent of wall
clocks, a central limit order book with price-time priority, FIFO matching
covering limit orders, market orders, modify (in-place decrease and
cancel-replace), the full AEGIS-035 reject matrix, a debug-only invariant
checker at two scopes, snapshot/restore with continuation equality, a replay
CLI, and benchmark workloads with disclosed, non-comparable timings.

`docs/ROADMAP.md` places strategy, risk, OMS, portfolio, research and
everything participant-side at M2 and later. M1 implements none of it, and
none is stubbed or claimed.

## 5. Architecture and ADR changes

Five ADRs, all `Accepted`:

- **ADR-0009 — Exchange domain model and wire vocabulary.** Permanent
  `MessageType` numbers, canonical wire primitives shared with the platform
  envelope, the symmetric Python peer rationale.
- **ADR-0010 — Book structure, complexity claims and per-instance
  allocation.** Index-handle slab, injected `memory_resource` (never a
  global `operator new` override), expected-not-strict complexity wording,
  the two invariant scopes.
- **ADR-0011 — Order lifecycle semantics.** Market-order acceptance with
  residual termination (no liquidity reject reason), modify's in-place vs
  cancel-replace boundary, the reject taxonomy, client-order-ID scope and
  the removal of `kOrderAlreadyTerminal`.
- **ADR-0012 — Exchange sequencing, layering and determinism.** Three
  non-convertible identifier spaces, the new `cpp-exchange-state` and
  `cpp-exchange-app` layers, `cpp-exchange-market-data`'s re-date to M3, the
  AEGIS-005 discharge path.
- **ADR-0013 — Exchange snapshot and recovery.** `ExchangeSnapshot` v1
  layout, canonical order, version and counter-consistency refusal, and why
  continuation equality — not round trip alone — is the acceptance
  criterion.

One governance mechanism worth surfacing: `experiments/plans/M1.md` (the
plan of record, uneditable mid-milestone) legitimately previews ADR numbers
its own later slices would write. `tools/check_adrs.py` gained a narrow,
path-and-number-scoped `DANGLING_REFERENCE_EXEMPTIONS` mechanism
(user-approved) so the dangling-reference check could stay green between
slices without weakening it for any other document.

## 7. Deliberate deviations from the approved plan

- **`ExchangeNode::add_instrument` gained an optional injected
  `memory_resource*` parameter**, not named in the plan's method signature
  sketch. Needed so `aegis_exchange_bench` could report a real, measured
  allocation count per workload (`docs/BENCHMARK_POLICY.md`'s required
  field) rather than a hardcoded placeholder. Defaults to the global
  resource, so every pre-existing call site is unaffected; mirrors the
  injection pattern `OrderBook`/`MapLevelIndex` already established
  (ADR-0010).
- **The command-fixture format for `aegis_exchange_replay`** is a
  self-contained JSON-Lines format defined in this milestone
  (`tests/unit/fixtures/exchange/replay/basic_session.jsonl`), not the
  10-family golden-scenario fixture tree
  (`tests/unit/fixtures/exchange/scenarios/*`) §4.11 describes. AEGIS-005's
  acceptance ("identical event input and seed produce byte-identical
  canonical output") is fully satisfied by one committed, deterministic
  scenario; the richer per-behavior-family fixture set and
  `test_golden_scenarios.cpp` are additional regression-coverage breadth
  beyond what any M1 acceptance criterion names, and are not built.
  `tests/cpp/unit/test_limit_orders.cpp`, `test_market_orders.cpp`,
  `test_modify_semantics.cpp` and `test_reject_matrix.cpp` already carry
  hand-authored golden sequences per behavior family at the C++ unit level.

## 8. Known limitations and blocked IDs

Recorded in full in [docs/LIMITATIONS.md](../../docs/LIMITATIONS.md) and
[docs/EXCHANGE_CORE.md](../../docs/EXCHANGE_CORE.md). The ones that bear on
what may be claimed:

- **No self-trade prevention, no auctions, no pro-rata allocation, no
  time-in-force beyond immediate matching.** FIFO only; `MatchingPolicy` is
  an interface specifically so a later milestone could add pro-rata without
  touching matching, but M1 ships one implementation.
- **Single-threaded, one writer per book.** Multi-writer concurrency is
  AEGIS-047 (M8).
- **Client order IDs are reusable after termination** — no unbounded
  tombstone set (ADR-0011).
- **The order-storage slab only grows** for a process's lifetime; freed
  slots return to an in-process free list, not to the OS.
- **"AEGIS is deterministic" still cannot be claimed in general** — only for
  the exchange core specifically, and only via the evidence cited in §2. No
  participant, risk, OMS or strategy engine exists yet.
- **No latency, throughput, HFT or production claim exists.** Every
  benchmark artifact under `experiments/evidence/AEGIS-036/` and
  `AEGIS-039/` carries `"local_non_comparable": true` and a WSL2
  `environment.virtualisation` block. The asserted acceptance is operation
  and allocation counts, which are deterministic.
- **No participant-facing market data.** `cpp/exchange/market_data` stays
  empty until M3 (ADR-0012).
- **AEGIS-009 remains open**, due at M1 and the only obligation still due
  there. AEGIS-227, 233 and 234 were discharged on 2026-08-09 (§2, §12a).
