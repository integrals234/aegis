# CV Claims Policy

Permitted:
- statements backed by committed test, experiment or benchmark artifacts;
- exact scope such as “exchange-style simulator” or “institutional-style research platform”;
- measured median and tail latency with methodology;
- out-of-sample, net-of-cost strategy results with dates and assumptions;
- honest negative findings.

Forbidden:
- “guarantees an interview” or “guaranteed profit”;
- “exact infrastructure used by every exchange/prop firm”;
- “all operations strict O(1)”;
- sub-500-ns or messages/second claims without evidence;
- Sharpe targets selected in advance;
- production/HFT/live claims for simulation-only code;
- calling correlation-based divergence statistical arbitrage without stationarity/relationship validation.

Before adding a number to the CV:
1. locate its evidence file;
2. rerun the command;
3. record environment and Git commit;
4. have the spec-auditor verify the wording;
5. add the evidence path to the relevant requirement status.
